What is this?

Stop forgetting what you own

You have a wardrobe full of clothes you love. You wear the same five things. Sound familiar? The Shimmer Strip surfaces combinations you'd never think of, tracks where everything lives, and lets your AI pick outfits based on the actual weather and your actual wardrobe.

💅

The Anchor Item Concept

No commercial wardrobe app understands "anchor items" that aren't clothing. Shimmer Strip lets your AI see your nail polish, your watch, your bag — and build the week's outfits around what you're already wearing. Incredibly practical. Nobody else does this.

🏠

True Location Intelligence

Other apps filter by season. Shimmer Strip knows your striped jumper is in the attic, your partner's wardrobe is inaccessible at night, and your winter coat is in storage. AI only suggests what you can actually reach, right now.

🔓

No Monetisation Walls

Most wardrobe apps gained popularity by being free, then limited users to 100 items. Shimmer Strip is open source, self-hosted, and free forever. Your wardrobe shouldn't have a paywall.
